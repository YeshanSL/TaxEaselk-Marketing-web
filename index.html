FAQ

Boundaries, answered.

Does V1 calculate my corporate tax?
No. V1 is deliberately not a tax engine. It focuses on collecting, organizing, and reviewing the documents required for tax work. Tax calculation is on the V2 roadmap.
Can I submit returns to the IRD through TaxEaseLK?
No, return generation and e-filing are explicitly out of scope for V1. The platform stops at auditor acceptance of the document package.
How does the auditor collaboration work?
You submit a complete package, the auditor reviews it, requests missing or corrected docs if needed via the request-response loop, and accepts the package when complete. Everything is tracked in history.
Are other tax types supported?
No. V1 handles only the corporate tax document workflow for Pvt Ltd companies. VAT, PAYE, and other taxes are not supported in this version.
