How It Works

Six steps. One compliant filing.

From onboarding to IRD acknowledgment — typically under 24 hours.

01

Onboarding

Register, pick your language, complete a guided profile — industry, VAT/SSCL status, financial year.

02

Document Upload

Upload income statements, T10s, VAT returns. OCR extracts the figures automatically.

03

Tax Calculation

Calculates using current IRD rates, and prepares a draft return.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a credit card to try TaxEaseLK?
No. The Free plan and 14-day trials on paid tiers require no credit card. You only enter payment details when you decide to upgrade to a paid subscription.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. You can cancel anytime from your dashboard. Annual plans remain active until the end of the billing period and won't auto-renew. No hidden cancellation fees.
How secure is my financial data?
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We use JWT with OAuth 2.0 for authentication and host on SOC 2-compatible AWS infrastructure.
Is TaxEaseLK an official IRD product?
No. TaxEaseLK is a private-sector platform that submits approved returns to the IRD e-Services Portal on your behalf — the only legal submission channel.
What tax types are supported?
The MVP covers Corporate Income Tax (CIT) and VAT. Phase 2 adds SSCL, WHT, and SET modules.
